## 🔄 Lead Capture Flow
1. User selects plan on homepage
2. Redirected to `/contato?plan=<planName>` with pre-selected plan
3. Fills contact form (name, email, phone, plan selection)
4. Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA verification (server-side)
5. Lead stored in Firebase Firestore via `/api/leads`
6. Redirected to `/thanks` with Cal.com scheduling embed
7. Cal.com meeting: "tecfydev/briefing-sem-modestia" (30min session)
